Getting started

Create a league from the Leagues tab, then share the invite code with friends so they can join. Each matchday, pick one team you think won't lose — an outright loss eliminates you, a draw keeps you alive. See the in-app Rules tab for full details on scoring, buy-backs, and tie-breaks.

Common questions

I missed a pick — what happens? If a matchday locks and you never submitted a pick, you're eliminated, the same as if your pick had lost.

Can I get back in after being eliminated? Only if your league admin enables a buy-back and grants it to you. This allows one reinstatement per season.

How do I delete my account? Go to Profile → Delete Account inside the app. See our Privacy Policy for details on what happens to your data.

I'm not receiving pick reminders. Check that notifications are enabled for Survivor Football in your device Settings.
